Read completed workout sets, weights, reps and recorded RPE from Xunji. Compare the same exercise across sessions.

Recovery

Daily reflection

Review a daily summary, add energy and soreness check-ins, and export a report. AI-written interpretation is optional.

A public introduction. A local personal app.

This website contains only product information and policies. It does not sign you into Oura, collect API keys or display personal health records. The current MVP runs on your computer and stores connected data locally.

How the connection works

  1. Run the local application from the source repository.
  2. Connect your own Xunji account and authorize Oura through its OAuth consent screen.
  3. Sync recent records and review your report. Enable an external AI provider only if you choose to send a report summary.

Recovery metrics are context for reflection, not a diagnosis or a measurement of whether a specific muscle is ready to train. The prototype can draft and follow up local training plans from preferences and feedback, and can fit experimental personal fatigue estimates when data and validation requirements are met. Accepted-plan revisions are shown separately.
