3. Reports and limitations

Reports may be incomplete, delayed or incorrect because of missing logs, sensor limitations, synchronization timing, inconsistent units, software errors or AI output. The application does not diagnose, prevent or treat medical conditions and may generate local training-plan drafts and update model estimates from feedback; these are experimental suggestions to review, not validated prescriptions. Its content is not a substitute for individualized professional advice. Seek appropriate qualified help for medical concerns rather than relying on a generated report.
